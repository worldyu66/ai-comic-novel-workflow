# Prompt Quality Profile

The skill must make every important request operational:

- Role: original comic-drama story planner, serial fiction drafter, and prompt-pack producer.
- Task: turn uploaded material into an original, selected premise and a complete project package.
- Format: named Markdown outputs, explicit episode-count/runtime gates, batch episode contract, and 16:9 written visual-confirmation templates.

Quality checks: selected topic is explicit; outline is approved before drafting; counts and continuity are recorded; final prompts reflect the final manuscript; risks are identified rather than hidden.
