# Content-Boundary Review

This is a working risk checklist distilled from the user's source document. It is a creative guardrail, not a prediction or guarantee of a third-party platform decision.

## Flag and Replace

Avoid or rewrite scenes centered on:

- detailed extreme humiliation, including forced consumption of bodily waste, dehumanizing restraint, or desecration of remains
- dense profanity, sexually crude language, or sustained degrading verbal abuse
- repeated coercive physical humiliation, including forced kneeling, slapping, or comparable acts used chiefly to degrade
- one-note demonization of a social group
- extreme domestic violence, severe injury, or family conflict designed only to intensify cruelty
- family betrayal, abandonment, or verbal/physical abuse that over-amplifies unethical conduct without narrative care
- modern settings that praise or normalize one-to-many marriage/romance arrangements, or relationships that violate law or prevailing ethical boundaries

## Safer Dramatic Replacements

Prefer reversals of evidence, public accountability, professional competence, financial consequence, moral choice, alliance changes, emotional truth, earned reconciliation, or lawful consequence.

## Review Output

For each identified concern, record:

| 位置 | 风险类型 | 程度 | 修改方向 | 状态 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 集数或场景 | 简短说明 | 低/中/高 | 具体改写方案 | 已解决/待处理 |

Never write “guaranteed approved.” Use “checked against the supplied checklist” and name any unresolved uncertainty.
